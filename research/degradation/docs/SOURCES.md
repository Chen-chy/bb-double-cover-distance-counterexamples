# Source literature and inherited project inputs

This file indexes references already cited by the research. No external source was fetched, rechecked, or added as new research during the export. The archived proofs remain authoritative about which outside result they use and how.

The principal covering convention is attributed to Benjamin C. B. Symons, Abhishek Rajput, and Dan E. Browne, *Sequences of Bivariate Bicycle Codes from Covering Graphs*, arXiv:2511.13560v3, especially Theorem 3.1 and the BB chain-complex conventions. The original notes use both the versioned HTML reference `https://arxiv.org/html/2511.13560v3` and unversioned abstract links. This export preserves those references without silently replacing the cited version.

The rank-one note also cites R. Wang and L. P. Pryadko, *Distance bounds for generalized bicycle codes*, arXiv:2203.17216, for comparison with GB conventions, not as the source of its parity or stabilization results.

The Stacks Project is cited for standard homological and algebraic facts, including Tag 0111 (long exact homology sequence), Tag 062D (regular/Koszul sequences), and Tag 0BYA in the earlier structural report. The original source notes identify their own dependence on these statements.

The structural argument/report also refers to Newton-polytope factorization material (`arXiv:math/0012099`) and the cited lattice lecture notes for Minkowski's second theorem. Exact links already appearing in the original files or public report are retained there. External PDFs and papers were not present as local input files and have not been bundled.

The original task attachments are [Pasted text.txt](../context/input/Pasted%20text.txt) and [Pasted markdown.md](../context/input/Pasted%20markdown.md). They include assumptions and inherited results from other research threads. They are context sources, not substitutes for missing independent verification packages; see [known gaps](KNOWN_GAPS.md).
