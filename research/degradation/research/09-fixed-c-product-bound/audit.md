2. **Full optimization:** The systematic and single-coset forms include every admissible Laurent message and every universal boundary adjustment. Translations, not an untracked basis change, identify the 12 unit classes.

5. **Determinant bound:** Each fundamental cycle uses any selected qubit at most once. 6. **Minima:** Constant-message values are upper bounds only. Their exact support counts are not asserted to be optimized distances. The quadratic comparison uses a product lower bound applying to every admissible base message.
7. **Finite periods:** Actual distance equalities are invoked only in the inherited stabilized regime. No compact-only argument is used to exclude short-period winding logicals.
8. **Negative control:** Its full minima are exact, but its lifted numerator has five supported terms. The extra sheet pair cancels on projection and violates termwise strict-trinomial matching. It is not a target counterexample.
9. **What remains:** No proof or full-minimum counterexample to factor three; no universal linear constant; no resolution of global finiteness of C_*.
