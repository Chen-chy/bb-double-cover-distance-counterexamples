5. Multiplication by the period is checked to be a nonzerodivisor, including nonsplit seams.

9. The single-parity weight estimate includes the physical shear and both s sheets.

12. The all-degree distance certificate includes a homology label. A nonzero stabilizer is not accepted as a logical.
