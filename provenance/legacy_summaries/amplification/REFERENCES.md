# References and external dependencies already cited in the research

This is an archival bibliography drawn from the retained reports and visible
conversation, not a fresh literature review. Versions and citations are preserved
as used there. Full article snapshots were not available in this export runtime,
and these links were not newly checked for currency.

The primary cover definition, term-matching convention, and original conjecture
are attributed to Benjamin C. B. Symons, Abhishek Rajput, and Dan E. Browne,
*Sequences of Bivariate Bicycle Codes from Covering Graphs*,
[arXiv:2511.13560v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13560v3), especially Theorem 3.1 and
the projection/transfer and dimension results. The research's 7/3 construction
must not be attributed to that paper.

The later general geometric bounds use the arbitrary-boundary quotient-lattice
theorem of Arnault, Gaborit, Rozendaal, Saussay, and Zémor,
[arXiv:2502.04995v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04995v2), especially Theorem 3.6
and its two-block application. The recorded cubic f(d) and square-root Gamma(N)
corollaries depend on this external input as stated in rounds 05 and 06.

The universal-versus-specialized homology warning cites the Stacks Project,
[Tag 061Y](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/061Y) and
[Tag 061Z](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/061Z). The explicit non-BB
specialization counterexample is proved inside the compact-survival report.

Other earlier contextual references are retained here because they explain
examined routes and rejected transfers of classical-code results:

bibliography. Several earlier exploratory web references were not copied as full
sources; material assertions should be read with the precise source and scope
in the corresponding report.
