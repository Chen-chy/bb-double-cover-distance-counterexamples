# Coverage, recovered material, and known gaps

“Self-contained” here means that the available proof/certificate bundles and
their retained audit paths run without the old workspace. It does not mean that
files missing from this session have been recreated with authenticated original
bytes. This document prevents that distinction from being lost on GitHub.

## Available and preserved

All eight mounted round bundles were inspected, decompressed, hashed, and checked
against their original manifests. They contain 267 file entries, representing
240 unique byte payloads. Every entry has a source-to-repository mapping. The
original source code, report text, certificates, data, and logs are retained
byte-for-byte at a canonical storage path.

Eight separately attached reports and four separately attached source files
matched bundle payloads exactly. They are mapped to the same canonical copies,
not stored again. Two mounted research briefs are retained verbatim. All binary
closure/Bellman certificates, gzip search records, old search-engine versions,
partial probes, and original cutoff flags are included.

The source inventory records the ZIP hashes and every original member path.
The original ZIP containers are not stored alongside their extracted contents;
that would add exact redundancy without improving runnable reproduction.
`tools/materialize.py` recreates the original **file contents/layout**, not a
byte-identical compressed ZIP container.

## What was recovered from the visible conversation

`history/01_initial_family_and_baseline.md` and
`history/02_distance4_and_transfer_metric.md` preserve the materially relevant
early arguments, exact families, failed routes, tables, and scope cautions as
conversation-derived reading copies. They are not the missing original reports.
The initial d=2 discussion still refers to an originally linked full idempotent
calculation that is not available as a file; downstream reports explicitly treat
that small-case result as inherited input.

Complete visible source was transcribed for the early univariate algebra,
circuit enumerator, polynomial-oracle verifier, template/random search scripts,
both final d=4 enumeration implementations, and the exact transfer-metric family
verifier. Known visible final portability/capacity edits are documented in the
recovered-source READMEs. Their export hashes authenticate the transcription,
not original files. Bounded checks were rerun on the recovered seed and final
d=4/metric programs; the new records are separately labeled.

Preparatory one-off inspection scripts whose only outcome was superseded by the
complete final classifier are not reconstructed as additional duplicate code.
Their material conclusions are retained in the proof history. Original outputs
that were redacted or missing cannot be recovered from program text alone.
