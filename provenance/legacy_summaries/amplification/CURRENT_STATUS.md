## Global amplification problem

The class is binary strict-trinomial BB codes and term-matched double covers
with k>0. The best exact ratio established here is 14/6=7/3. The connected family
has

\[
L_j=126j+39,\quad A_j=1+x^{42j+8}+x^{84j+25},\quad
B_j=1+x^{42j+2}+x^{84j+13},
\]
\[
[[252j+78,4,6]]\longrightarrow[[504j+156,4,14]].
\]

The [early conversation-derived proof](../history/01_initial_family_and_baseline.md)
records the uniform construction and provenance limitations. The finite seed
has intact independent circuit and polynomial-oracle verifiers in
[round 03](../research/03_amplification_search/README.md). Later compact closure
certificates give separate fixed-strip realizations.

No finite universal upper bound on C_* is established. The thresholds 7/3, 5/2,
and 3 are all unresolved for the whole class. The inherited original factor-two
upper conjecture is contradicted by the seed; this does not by itself resolve
other claims in the source paper.

## Higher-rank compact problem: resolved in its stated regime

For an incidence presentation generated by the four check differences, free
rank at least two, and neither check supported in a finite subgroup, surviving
compact homology forces a torsion–Frobenius normal form

\[
A=1+X+Y,\qquad B=1+hX^{2^s}+kY^{2^s}.
\]

The [compact-survival theorem](../research/05_compact_survival/REPORT.md) supplies
one compact cover class surviving all relevant finite quotients. It is not a
general base-change theorem for arbitrary Koszul pairs.

If T_2 has invariant factors 2^a>=2^b and o=|T_odd|, the
[exact compact metric theorem](../research/06_linear_compact_replacement/REPORT.md)
gives

\[
\delta_{\rm comp}=o\,2^{a-b}3^b(3^s+1),\qquad
\widetilde\delta_{\rm comp}/\delta_{\rm comp}\in\{1,3/2,2\}.
\]

The minimizing universal cycle generates the compact module. This is factor two
in the stated higher-rank regime, not in all BB covers or all finite logical
sectors. The cited report explains the minimum-circuit application.

## Rank-one reductions and current slope-(2,1) obstruction

The [rank-one report](../research/07_rank_one_resonance/REPORT.md) proves factor two
for one-sided defects and most two-sided parity patterns, and reduces the rest
to odd-order resonance. Its divided-Euler construction gives factor 2m for each
fixed primitive slope, where m=max(|p|,|q|,|p-q|). At slope (2,1), the inherited
bound is therefore four. Large-period realization equates full compact and
actual finite distances once winding weights are excluded; a lower bound only
for a selected sector or fixed polynomial message is insufficient.

The latest objective is

\[
\widehat\Delta\le3\Delta
\]

for the **full** compact minimum at (2,1). Neither this inequality nor the
stronger half-weight completion lemma is settled. The exact graph formulation,
complete common-message equations, and source-free independent-set reduction
are in [rounds 09–10](../research/10_nonlocal_exchange/REPORT.md).

Current negative controls are substantive but do not give a factor-three
counterexample. The record has full compact minimum 14, not its sector-restricted
minimum 16. Its Pareto frontier is {(12,1),(14,0)}. A fixed-message minimum 74N
stays in a universal class whose minimum is 14. The conflict graph can contain a
triangle despite bipartiteness before contraction.

The precise next target is the packing-deficit transfer lemma in
[Open problems](OPEN_PROBLEMS.md), not a new enumeration or a fixed-message
majority rule.
